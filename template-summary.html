Summary

Most eCommerce platforms that miss revenue targets in their first 90 days didn't fail because of bad products or weak marketing. They failed because speed replaced strategy at the worst possible moment: go-live.

This blog examines why organizations bypass the planning phase and identifies the "hidden taxes" paid later through technical debt and brand damage. By pivoting from "Fast Deployment" to "Strategic Launching" and utilizing the specialized expertise of partners like Virtina, companies can transition from high-risk technical builds to stable, high-ROI systems.
