# Content brief

## Thesis

Adult day care is the most overlooked option on the care menu, and it is the cheapest one by a wide margin.
At a 2025 national median of $95 a day it runs roughly $24,700 a year, against $74,400 for assisted living
and $80,080 for full-time care at home. It is also the only option that solves the caregiver's actual
problem, which is the hours between 8am and 5pm on a weekday.

## Why this reader is searching

The trigger is not a fall or a diagnosis. It is a job. The reader cannot leave a parent alone all day and
cannot afford to stop working. They are looking for a supervised place their parent can go, and they do not
yet know this category exists by name. Search intent is informational, so the post explains before it sells.

## Angle

Frame the whole post around the working caregiver's weekday, not around the care industry's taxonomy.
Lead with what it costs relative to the alternatives, because that is the fact most likely to change the
reader's shortlist. Stay neutral: the post must be equally usable by a reader who concludes adult day care
is wrong for their parent, which is why "Who Adult Day Care Helps Most" names the cases where it does not fit.
