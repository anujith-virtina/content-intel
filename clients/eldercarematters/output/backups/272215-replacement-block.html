How Families Pay for Respite Care

Most families start by paying privately. Several other payers exist, and each one has narrow rules about who qualifies.

Medicare does not pay for ongoing custodial respite care. The one exception is the hospice benefit, which includes short inpatient respite stays so you can rest. Medicaid waivers, the VA, and your Area Agency on Aging each fund respite in different ways.

Eligibility for Medicaid is a legal and financial question. Speak to an elder law attorney before you move any money.
