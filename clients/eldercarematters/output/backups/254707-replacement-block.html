How to Choose the Right Geriatric Care Manager

Choosing the right care manager matters more than most families expect. The title is not a licensed one in most states, so credentials, references, and a clear conflict-of-interest answer do the real work.

Look for a recognised certification and an underlying professional licence. Ask who covers when they are away, and ask directly whether they receive referral fees from any community or agency they recommend.

The credentials that carry weight, the questions to ask, and the red flags worth walking away from are covered in this guide to choosing a geriatric care manager.
