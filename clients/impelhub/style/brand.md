# Brand — ImpelHub

## Positioning
ImpelHub is a decision filter, not a strategy generator. We eliminate low-impact moves and tell founders what NOT to do, with reasons. Built for "Guardians of Growth" — founders, CEOs, and growth leaders who own the revenue number personally.

Big League Strategy. SMB Velocity. Speed to ROI.

In 8-10 days and ~60 minutes of the customer's time, ImpelHub builds a custom AI-driven Business Brain that delivers playbooks and execution plans, not just reports.

## Things never to say
- "Just another consulting firm" — we are not consulting
- "Replaces your team" — we augment, not replace
- "AI-powered" as filler — be specific
- "Schedule a discovery call" gates — we offer self-serve + free strategy audits
- Negative comparisons to specific named competitors
