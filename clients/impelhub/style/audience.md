# Audience — ImpelHub

## Primary persona
Founders, CEOs, and growth leaders at SMB-to-mid-market companies ($500K-$50M revenue, 5-200 employees). Across B2B and B2C, with strong B2B SaaS, ecommerce, services, and supply chain representation.

## Self-identifier
"Guardians of Growth" — they personally own the revenue number. Not delegated. Not an MBB engagement. They have to ship.

## What they already know
- Their business inside out
- Why generic consulting doesn't work for them (too slow, too expensive)
- That AI tools can speed up work, but most produce generic outputs
- The pain of decision paralysis when 50 strategies look equally valid

## What they don't know or get wrong
- They confuse GTM strategy with growth strategy (per ImpelHub blog)
- They think product-market fit is binary (it's signal-based)
- They underestimate how much fractional leadership can replace full-time hires early
- They overinvest in tools that don't move the lever

## Reading context
Late night or early morning founder hours. 5-10 minutes max. Reading because they're stuck on a specific decision: "Do I hire a sales rep now or wait?" "Is this PMF or am I fooling myself?" "Should I rebrand or double down?"

## What they hate
- Generic frameworks they've seen on LinkedIn 100 times
- "Schedule a 30-min discovery call" before any value
- Consultant fluff and academic abstractions
- Tools that show data without telling them what to do
- AI outputs that hallucinate or feel generic

## Reading level
Industry-fluent. Comfortable with: ICP, GTM, ACV, ARR, CAC, LTV, PMF, runway, NRR, churn, ICP scoring, BANT, MEDDIC. No need to define common SaaS or B2B acronyms.
