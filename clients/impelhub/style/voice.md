# Voice — ImpelHub

## Tone
Direct, confident, decision-filter framing. Anti-fluff, anti-consultant-speak, anti-buzzword. Frames decisions in clear terms. Comfortable telling the reader what NOT to do, with reasons.

## Signature voice patterns from the live site
- "ImpelHub is a decision filter. Not a strategy generator. We eliminate low-impact moves and tell you what not to do, with reasons."
- "Stop chasing low-impact ideas. Stop copying competitors blindly. Stop endless debates and ship a plan."
- "Built for Guardians of Growth — the founders, CEOs, and growth leaders who own the number."
- "You carry the revenue target personally. You don't have an MBB budget, and you don't have time for consultants who take six months to produce a PowerPoint."
- "When AI Levels the Playing Field, Strategy Becomes the Edge."
- "Speed to ROI"
- "10 days. 60 minutes of your time."

## Person
Second person ("you", "your") dominant. First person plural ("we") when speaking as the brand. No third-person filler.

## Sentence structure
Short, punchy declarative statements. Three-word punches followed by 12-15 word explanations. Use period for emphasis. Like this. Avoid hedging.

## Punctuation
- Em dashes BANNED (replace with periods, commas, hyphens)
- Semicolons avoided
- Oxford comma yes
- Exclamation marks: very rare, only for genuine punch
- Trademark symbols where ImpelHub uses them: Impeleration™, BattleBoard™

## Banned words and phrases
AI tells (never use): delve, leverage, navigate (verb), realm, landscape, ecosystem, "in today's fast-paced world", "it's important to note", "in conclusion", "innovative solutions"

Consultant-speak banned (these are the OPPOSITE of ImpelHub voice):
- "synergize", "transformative journey", "thought partnership"
- "best-in-class", "world-class", "industry-leading"
- "let's circle back", "at the end of the day"
- "stakeholder alignment", "value proposition workshop"
- Long McKinsey-style hedging phrases

Hype banned: revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, "transform your..."

## Phrases ImpelHub OWNS (use freely)
- "Guardians of Growth"
- "Speed to ROI"
- "Decision filter"
- "Big League Strategy. SMB Velocity."
- "Stop chasing low-impact ideas"
- "Business Brain"
- "Impeleration™ Framework"
- "Built for founders who own the number"

## Formatting
- Sentence case headings (not Title Case)
- Bold for genuine emphasis
- TL;DR / Key takeaways pattern at top of long articles (ImpelHub blogs use this consistently)
- No emoji in blog body

## What a good ImpelHub opening looks like
Good: "Most B2B founders confuse GTM with growth. They buy a GTM strategy when what they need is a growth strategy. The two solve different problems."

Bad: "In today's competitive B2B landscape, founders face many challenges when it comes to going to market..."

## Vocabulary
Always use:
- "founder", "CEO", "growth leader" (specific audience labels)
- "playbook" (the deliverable)
- "decision filter" (the positioning)
- "impact × innovation score" (proprietary scoring)
- "Business Brain" (the AI context layer)

Never use:
- "solutions" as filler
- "Empower" / "enable" as filler verbs
- Generic "businesses" when "founders" or "CEOs" is more specific
