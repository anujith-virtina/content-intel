**Search intent:** Decision-intent informational. The founder is not just researching — they are stuck on an operational problem. They have churn. They don't know whether it is a product problem, a fit problem, or an onboarding problem. They need a diagnostic first, then a prioritized action sequence.

---

### Why Candidate 1 beats Candidate 2

1. **Less crowded SERP.** PLG vs SLG has 9+ well-resourced posts in 2026, several from $50M+ product companies with massive backlink profiles. Churn reduction has coverage but the early-stage founder angle is systematically missing across all top results.

2. **Cleaner gap.** Every churn article is written for CS teams, not for solo founders running both product and sales. ImpelHub's "decision filter" voice directly answers "what do I fix first?" — a framing no competitor uses.

3. **Inventory uniqueness is stronger.** PLG vs SLG has some proximity risk to ImpelHub's GTM cluster (11 posts), even if none covers this exact angle. Churn is cleanly absent from all 50 posts.

4. **Founder search intent is sharper.** "My churn is high and I don't know why" is a more urgent, specific decision moment than "should I be PLG or SLG?" The churn question has immediate financial stakes that create high-intent search.

5. **ImpelHub voice fits perfectly.** The "decision filter" positioning is made for a topic with exactly this structure: here is the diagnostic, here is the prioritization logic, here is what most founders do wrong (fix symptoms not root causes).

---

### Why this is the right ImpelHub angle

ImpelHub's core promise is: eliminate low-impact moves and tell you what NOT to do. Churn reduction is typically treated as a 10-tip listicle. ImpelHub's angle flips this — instead of a list of tactics, the post is a diagnostic-first framework: identify the root cause first, then apply the right fix. That is the decision filter in action.

The article should NOT be titled "10 ways to reduce churn." It should be titled something like: "Your B2B SaaS churn is lying to you: how to find the real root cause before you fix the wrong thing."

- Format D ("Should I X or Y?" / "How to decide between X and Y") fits the diagnostic-first structure perfectly
- Bullet-based criteria at each decision step are appropriate for churn root cause identification
- Format A (standard explanatory) is used in the most recent reference post (12356) — rotating to Format D provides structural uniqueness
