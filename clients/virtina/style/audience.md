# Audience — Virtina

## Who reads this

**Primary: B2B ecommerce leaders**
- Roles: VP eCommerce, Director of Digital, Head of eCommerce, Ecommerce Manager
- Companies: Manufacturers, distributors, wholesalers — $5M–$500M+ revenue
- Verticals: Industrial, medical, automotive, electrical, food & beverage, fashion, jewelry
- Platforms: Magento, BigCommerce, WooCommerce, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Vtex

**Secondary: B2C ecommerce founders and operators**
- Roles: Founder, CEO, COO, Head of Ecommerce
- Companies: DTC brands and retailers — $1M–$50M ARR
- Platforms: Shopify, BigCommerce, WooCommerce; some on legacy platforms (Volusion, Magento 1) considering migration

## What they already know

- Their platform's strengths and limitations — deeply
- Core ecommerce vocabulary: GMV, AOV, CR, CAC, LTV, ROAS
- That ecommerce is hard — don't explain it
- Common platforms and their broad use cases
- Common integrations: ERPs, PIMs, ESPs, 3PLs, payment, tax

## What they actually struggle with

- Diagnosing where the store is bleeding money (checkout, speed, integrations, plugin conflicts)
- Fix vs. migrate decisions — and to what platform
- Scaling B2B workflows: complex pricing, quotes, customer-specific catalogs, approval flows
- Page speed and Core Web Vitals hurting conversion and SEO
- Choosing between in-house dev, freelancers, or an agency
- Cutting through vendor BS to find what's actually worth doing

## Reading context

- Mostly desktop, during work hours while investigating a problem
- Found via Google: searches like "magento 2 to shopify migration cost", "B2B quote workflow plugin", "fix slow woocommerce site"
- Skim-first readers — headline + opening must earn the full read
- 5–15 minutes max for long-form

## What they hate

- Generic "Top 10 platforms" listicles with no opinion
- Vendor pitches disguised as editorial
- Surface-level think pieces that don't help them ship
- "It depends!" answers that never explain when
- Hype words: revolutionary, game-changing, transform
- Corporate stock-photo writing with no real expertise

## Reading level

- Industry-fluent — acronyms OK after first use
- Technical terms (API, GraphQL, headless, PWA, schema, OMS) used freely
- Platform-specific terminology fine without explanation when context is clear
