## Article structure every Virtina piece should follow

1. Hero image (1309×500 standard)
2. H1 title in sentence case
3. Author byline + category tags + updated date
4. **Summary block** — 2–3 sentence scenario or thesis; opens with a real situation, not a definition
5. **Introduction** — 1–2 paragraphs setting up the problem
6. **Table of Contents** with anchor links to all H2s
7. **Body sections** — H2 for main sections, H3 for sub-sections; contextual images every 3–4 sections
8. **"People Also Ask"** — 3–4 short Q&As (SERP optimization)
9. **Conclusion** — short, action-oriented
10. **FAQ section** — 6–8 longer, conversational Q&As at the bottom
11. **Author bio** with photo and credentials
12. Related Blogs / Recent Blogs sidebar (auto-generated — not required in draft)

---

## Voice patterns to mirror

- Open with a scenario, not a definition. ("Think about running a small online shop selling dumbbells..." not "Agentic AI is defined as...")
- Use "I've seen" or first-person observation when sharing examples — gives authority without bragging
- Mix short punchy sentences with longer explanatory ones for rhythm
- Drop in concrete numbers (10–12 hours saved, 18% repeat purchase increase, $20–50/month tools)
- Name specific tools and platforms (Shopify Magic, LimeSpot, Rebuy, ImpelHub) instead of "various AI tools"
- Use "your team" / "your store" — keeps it operator-focused
- Bold key phrases sparingly within paragraphs for scanability
- End sections with a forward-pointing line that hints at the next section

## Length guidance

| Format | Word count |
|---|---|
| Pillar guides | 2,500–3,500 words |
| Standard articles | 1,500–2,500 words |
| Opinion / contrarian pieces | 1,200–1,800 words |

All pieces include Summary, TOC, and FAQ blocks regardless of length.

---

## Visual / design requirements

Creator should flag these in every draft even when not generating images:

- **Hero image concept:** photorealistic, business-context (people working, dashboards, real environments — not abstract tech graphics). Standard size: 1309×500.
- **Section images:** illustrative scenarios matching the section topic
- **All images:** alt text written for SEO and accessibility

---

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- Listicles with no opinion ("Top 10 ecommerce platforms" without picking a winner)
- Generic intros ("In today's fast-paced ecommerce world...")
- Filler quotes from unnamed "industry experts"
- Stock-photo corporate writing
- "It depends" answers that never explain when
