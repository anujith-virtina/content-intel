# Brand — Virtina

## Positioning

> Virtina is the eCommerce solutions partner that strategizes, optimizes, and solves — we ship the plan and the fixes for B2B and B2C stores that are slow, broken, or stuck.

**Tagline:** STRATEGIZE · OPTIMIZE · SOLVE

Virtina is a **partner**, not a vendor — an extension of the client's team. Use "partner" when describing the relationship. "Agency" is acceptable but less preferred. Never "vendor."

## Things never to say

- "Revolutionary," "game-changing," "best-in-class," "cutting-edge," "world-class," "industry-leading"
- "Transform your eCommerce" / "transform your business" — vague hype
- "We're the best" — let credentials and numbers speak
- "Cheap" or "low cost" — we compete on results, not price
- Promises not backed by case studies or numbers
- Anything that reads like a vendor pitch
- Generic platform comparisons with no opinion ("it depends!" with no guidance)
- Blanket dismissals of platforms — criticism must be use-case specific, not general
- "Reach out" — say "contact" or "talk to us"
- "Solutions" as filler — "eCommerce solutions" is fine, "innovative solutions" is not
- "Synergize," "leverage," "unlock value"
- "In conclusion," "to summarize"

## Themes we own

1. B2B eCommerce done right — complex pricing, quote workflows, multi-tier customer catalogs, ERP integration
2. Replatforming and migration without breaking what works
3. Performance and Core Web Vitals as a revenue lever, not a tech metric
4. CRO grounded in actual user behavior, not generic best-practice lists
5. Platform selection for the actual use case (Shopify vs Magento vs BigCommerce, headless vs traditional, SaaS vs open source)
6. Fixing revenue-leaking issues fast — checkout, integrations, speed, plugin conflicts

## Themes to avoid

- Pure marketing topics not tied to commerce platforms (general SEO theory, generic email marketing)
- Crypto / NFT / Web3 commerce
- Generic AI hype not grounded in eCommerce-specific use cases
- DTC/D2C lifestyle takes
- Anything political

## Voice extensions for the brand

Always say:
- "Partner" (not "vendor") when describing the relationship
- "Ship" — we ship plans, fixes, integrations; action-oriented
- "Stop the bleeding" / "fix what's broken" — Virtina language for urgent issues
- Specific platform names (Magento, BigCommerce, etc.) instead of "your eCommerce platform"
