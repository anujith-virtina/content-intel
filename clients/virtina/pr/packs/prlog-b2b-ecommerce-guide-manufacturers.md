## Body (paste as plain text)

KING OF PRUSSIA, PA, November 2, 2026 - Virtina, an eCommerce partner for B2B and B2C brands, has released a guide for manufacturers who want to sell online. The B2B Ecommerce Development Guide for Manufacturers is a free article on the Virtina website: https://virtina.com/b2b-ecommerce-development-guide-manufacturers/

This is a guide publication, not a product launch. It is written for manufacturers planning a B2B online store.

The guide starts with how manufacturers sell. Prices differ by customer. Many orders begin as quote requests. Account data sits in an ERP system. It then lists the features a B2B store needs: customer-specific pricing, quote requests, reorder tools, ERP integration, and account permissions.

It also explains how B2B buyers behave online and how to organize a product catalog.

On quotes, the guide describes taking requests by form or chat. Standard items can get an instant answer. Custom parts go to a person. On payment, it explains that net terms need a credit check and approval before a buyer is offered them.

The guide compares four platforms: WooCommerce, Adobe Commerce, BigCommerce, and Shopify Plus. It also covers how pricing visibility affects sales and search. Prices can be public, hidden, or shown as a starting price.

For rollout, the guide gives four steps:

1. Map current buyers, products, and pricing rules.
2. Run a pilot with a few key accounts.
3. Add quotes, reorders, and integrations in phases.
4. Train the sales team and measure adoption.

It includes an illustrative example of a manufacturer with 4,000 parts and 300 active accounts. The example starts with a pilot of twenty large accounts.

The takeaway for manufacturers: you do not have to rebuild everything at once. Start with current buyers and processes, then add features in phases.
