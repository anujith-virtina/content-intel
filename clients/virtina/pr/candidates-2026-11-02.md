## Key finding

True press-release distribution on high-DR sites is almost always paid. Free high-DR options are owned platforms (Medium, LinkedIn) and trade outlets that run contributed or vendor news. Several well-known ecommerce outlets explicitly refuse press releases.

## Human-only steps

1. Quality Digest: fill the form and upload the file. Check for a login prompt or CAPTCHA at submit time.
2. Medium: create account (email verification), import story from the canonical URL, then submit to a publication.
3. LinkedIn: publish from the signed-in profile, then link back to virtina.com.
4. Pitch-only outlets (Business 2 Community, E-Commerce Nation, Ecommerce CEO): email or form. Each needs its own original article, not this release. NEEDS USER: decide whether to write those.
