# Virtina Publishes B2B Ecommerce Development Guide for Manufacturers

**The guide covers features, platforms, quotes, ERP integration, net terms, and a phased rollout.**

KING OF PRUSSIA, PA, October 30, 2026 - Virtina, an eCommerce partner for B2B and B2C brands, has published the B2B Ecommerce Development Guide for Manufacturers. The guide explains how a manufacturer can plan, build, and launch a B2B online store. It is available now at https://virtina.com/b2b-ecommerce-development-guide-manufacturers/.

Manufacturers sell differently from retailers. Prices vary by customer, orders often begin as quotes, and account data lives in an ERP. The guide starts from those facts.

It names the core features a B2B store needs: customer-specific pricing, quote requests, reorder tools, ERP integration, and account permissions. It also covers how B2B buyers behave online and how to structure a product catalog.

On quotes, the guide describes collecting requests by form or chat. Standard items can be answered instantly. Custom parts go to a person. On payments, it notes that net terms need credit checks and approval before a buyer is offered them.

The guide compares four platforms: WooCommerce, Adobe Commerce, BigCommerce, and Shopify Plus. It also looks at how pricing visibility (public, hidden, or starting price) affects both sales and SEO.

For the rollout, the guide gives four steps:

1. Map current buyers, products, and pricing rules.
2. Launch a pilot with a few key accounts.
3. Add quotes, reorders, and integrations in phases.
4. Train the sales team and measure adoption.

An example in the guide follows a manufacturer with 4,000 parts and 300 active accounts. It starts with a pilot of twenty large accounts. Over time, routine orders move online and sales reps focus on large accounts.

**What this means for merchants:** Manufacturers do not need to rebuild everything at once. The guide shows how to start with existing buyers and processes, then add features in phases.
