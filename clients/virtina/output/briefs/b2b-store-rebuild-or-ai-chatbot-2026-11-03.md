## 1. Thesis and angle

**Thesis:** A chatbot can only answer as well as the store and product data behind it, so fix the foundation first, add the answer layer second, and run both together when you can, because that is the fastest route.

**Why this angle, why now, why Virtina:**
- Unclaimed lane. No Virtina page argues sequencing. 43075 (`/redesign-or-rebuild/`) and 43036 are redesign-only with no AI. 41430 and 42068 sell chat with no rebuild-first argument. 41511 has data-before-AI but about quoting software.
- Competitors say "it depends" and use unsourced percentages. This post gives one plain order, two honest limits, a comparison table and five checks.
- Virtina owns the foundation (14+ years, 2,500+ projects). ChatSKU is the answer layer. The user explicitly wants ChatSKU as co-star (deliberate exception to the no cross-post rule).

### 7. How do you know where to start? (240 words)
- Direct answer first: run five checks and start with the first one that fails.
- Five quick checks (bullets, Template F, each one line plus a how to test):
  1. **Speed check:** open your three top product pages on a phone on mobile data; if you give up waiting, start with the store.
  2. **Search check:** search five real part numbers, one with a typo; if the right product is not on top, start with search and data.
  3. **Data check:** open ten random products; each needs a clear title, specs, unit and pack size. Missing fields mean the chat would guess.
  4. **Match check:** compare price and stock on the site with your ERP for five items. Any mismatch is foundation work.
  5. **Night check:** read the last seven days of inbox and form fills for messages that arrived after hours, and note how long each waited. Many waiting means the answer layer will pay off.
- H3 "How do you read the result?": checks 1 to 4 failing means foundation first; 1 to 4 passing and check 5 failing means add the answer layer now; failures on both means run both together, with the store work leading.
- H3 "What is the free Store + Catalog Review?": one short paragraph as a suggestion: a free look at store (speed, mobile, product data, search) and catalog (how ready it is for AI answers) with short written suggestions. No timeline, no "we will".
- Link: #6 outdated store conversions (under check 1 or 2).
