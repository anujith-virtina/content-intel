# Brief: surgical update of post 37791

**This is not a new post and not a rewrite.** It is an in-place edit of a live, ranking page. Post ID 37791. Status stays `publish`. Slug stays `/best-ecommerce-websites/`. No redirect. No new post.

## Thesis

> The honest global top 10 is not the one most pages publish, because the single metric everyone ranks by cannot see the biggest sellers on earth.

## Why this, why now, why us

- **Why this angle:** every page above Virtina on this SERP publishes a number next to each site and states no methodology, or states one and then breaks it. Virtina publishes zero numbers. Publishing one dated metric, naming its source, and then explaining that metric's own blind spot is the only defensible position left on the SERP, and it is the one an LLM will cite.
- **Why now:** Temu is the number 2 shopping site on earth and is not on the page at all. Shein listed in Hong Kong on 2026-09-01. Overstock's parent begins trading as Neighborhood Intelligence (Nasdaq NXH) from 2026-08-17. The page's data is from December 2024.
- **Why Virtina:** the audience file says readers hate "generic Top 10 listicles with no opinion". Every competitor is a neutral survey. Virtina can say what each ranking means for someone running a store, which is the only thing on this page that a proxy vendor or a wound-down Shopify property cannot copy.

## Format selection (MUST-FOLLOW-RULES section 11)

- **Chosen: Format C, listicle with opinions.**
- **Reason:** the page already is a ranked list and the query demands one. Format C is the only format that lets the ranked table carry the argument while each entry still takes a position.
- **Recently overused, therefore skipped:** the last published posts used Format E (42491, 42428), Format B (42441) and Format A (42391, 42393). Format E is at 2 of the last 10 and Format A is heavily used across the older inventory. Format C has not been used in the recent set.
- **Uniqueness:** confirmed against `published-posts-inventory.md`. No other Virtina post owns this keyword, this slug, or a global ranked-marketplace angle. 37791 is the target page itself, so checks 1 to 5 were applied to confirm no *other* post collides. Closest neighbours (36161 multi-vendor marketplace software, 41015 marketplace trap, 36505 stats roundup, 37887 m-commerce) all pass on angle. The slug and post ID do not change, so no new slug is created.

# 1. Title and H1 decision

## Recommendation: option (b), the hybrid H1. Change it.

**New H1 (sentence case, replaces the WordPress post title):**

`Top 10 online shopping sites in the world, plus 11 eCommerce websites worth knowing`

**New Yoast SEO title (58 chars, title case is correct for a SERP title):**

**New Yoast meta description (152 chars):**

`See the top 10 online shopping sites in the world, ranked by monthly visits (Semrush, July 2026), plus why Taobao and JD.com never appear in traffic lists.`

**New Yoast focus keyword (currently empty):**

`top 10 online shopping sites in world`

## Reasoning

The page sits at position 10 for a keyword that appears **nowhere** in its title, its H1, or its slug. That is the diagnosis, not a theory. It is ranking on domain strength and entity coverage alone. Every page ranking above it puts the number 10 and the word "world" in the title. Adding the exact phrase to the title and H1 is the single highest-leverage change available and it costs nothing structurally.

**Why not option (a), keep the H1 and bolt on an H2.** It leaves the primary defect uncorrected. Google is being asked to rank a page titled "Top 20 eCommerce Websites" for "top 10 online shopping sites in world". A buried H2 is a weaker relevance signal than the title element and the H1, and it would mean doing all the content work while withholding the one change most likely to move the ranking. This is the safe-looking option that wastes the effort.

**Why not option (c), a full retitle to the keyword.** The slug is `/best-ecommerce-websites/`. Existing backlinks and internal anchors point at it with "ecommerce websites" language, and the page presumably holds secondary rankings for that phrase set. A title with zero occurrence of "eCommerce websites" would break title-to-slug alignment and throw away relevance the page already owns for a gain it might not get. You do not have to choose. A hybrid gets the new keyword into the leading position, which is where it counts, and keeps the legacy phrase in the same string.

**On the number 11.** It is exact: 2 sites just outside the top 10 (Flipkart, Mercado Libre) plus 9 regional and category leaders. Total entity coverage becomes 21, so nothing is lost against the old "20". **If the creator changes the final entity list, the number in the H1 must change with it.** Do not leave a stale count in an H1 on a page whose whole argument is stat discipline.

## Also required

- The exact phrase "top 10 online shopping sites in the world" must appear in the **first 100 words** of the introduction, in a natural sentence.
- The phrase "eCommerce websites" must appear in the **first paragraph** as well as the H1.
- The old Yoast title "Top 20 eCommerce Websites: Leading the Online Shopping World" is replaced entirely.
