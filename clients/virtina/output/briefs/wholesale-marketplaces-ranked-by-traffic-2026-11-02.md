## The ranking metric (locked)

Ahrefs Site Explorer, United States filter, estimated monthly organic search visits, read 2026-11-02. Label every figure "Ahrefs estimate, US, 2 Nov 2026". Mandatory caveats, stated once in the method section and not repeated per entry:

- Modeled estimates, not exact counts.
- Organic search only. It undercounts direct, app, email and logged-in buying.
- Amazon Business is measured on the business.amazon.com subdomain only. Visits that begin on amazon.com are not counted, so its rank is understated. Say so.
- No site's figure separates business buyers from other visitors.
- Search visits are not revenue, orders, or supplier quality.
- US search favours US-facing sites. IndiaMART and Ankorstore serve India and Europe, so low US numbers say little about their size at home.
- The nine are a candidate set drawn from names that recur in competing lists. It is not an exhaustive census. Tundra and Wholesale Central recur in other lists but their traffic was not pulled, so they are not ranked (state this plainly, do not imply they rank lower).
