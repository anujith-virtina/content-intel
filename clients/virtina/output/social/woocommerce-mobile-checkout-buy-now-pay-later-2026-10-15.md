## LinkedIn

🚨 Your Black Friday sale will be decided on a phone screen.

Adobe's 2026 forecast puts mobile at 57.4% of online holiday spend. Buy now pay later is forecast at $21.3 billion, and 82% of it comes through mobile.

Here are 5 checkout fixes a WooCommerce store can make before the sale:

1️⃣ Wallets at the top
Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal let shoppers pay without typing a card number.

2️⃣ BNPL near the price
Add the pay in four message to the product page and the cart, close to the price.

3️⃣ Fewer fields
Make company name and phone number optional, and collapse address line 2.

4️⃣ Guest checkout
Offer account creation after the order, not before payment.

5️⃣ Test orders on real phones
Try Apple Pay in Safari on an iPhone and Google Pay in Chrome on Android.

💡 Most of these fixes are settings, small plugin changes and testing. Finish at least a week before Black Friday.

## Instagram

🚨 Can a shopper finish paying on a phone?

Most holiday spend happens on mobile, and a checkout that feels fine on your desktop can lose people at every tap.

Here are 5 fixes to make before Black Friday:

1️⃣ Wallets at the top
Apple Pay and Google Pay skip the card number and the address.

2️⃣ BNPL near the price
Show the pay in four message on the product page and the cart.

3️⃣ Fewer fields
Hide or make optional anything you do not need to ship the order.

4️⃣ Guest checkout
Let shoppers pay first and create an account after.

5️⃣ Test orders on real phones
Use Safari on an iPhone and Chrome on an Android.

💡 The cheapest fix on the list is the one most stores skip: a real test order.
