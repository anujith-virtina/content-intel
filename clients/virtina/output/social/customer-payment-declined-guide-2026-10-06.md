## LinkedIn (long post format)

A customer messages you: "My card didn't work, is something wrong with your store?"

Your stomach drops. Even though nothing may be wrong at all.

Here's what most WooCommerce store owners don't realize: a single declined card is almost never your fault. Expired cards, typos, low funds, a routine bank fraud check. Banks reject cards for their own reasons every day, on every website a cardholder visits. Your store just happened to be open when it happened.

So when should you actually worry?

Not when it happens once. When it happens to different customers, with different cards, from different banks, in the same short window. That pattern, not any single decline, is the real signal something in your setup needs a second look.

We just published a plain-English guide (no dev jargon, no acronyms) covering:

- The 5 most common reasons a single payment gets declined
- How to tell a one-off decline from a real pattern, with a real example
- A 2-minute self-check you can run yourself
- What a professional gateway review actually looks at, and when it's worth one

Written for store owners, not developers.
