## LinkedIn

Planning an online store build and not sure who to trust with it?

Most store owners hire a team before they know what to ask. The wrong choice shows up later, as rework, slow pages and a platform that doesn't fit. Virtina has delivered 2,500+ projects over 14+ years, and the same lessons keep repeating.

Here are 4 things to look for in an eCommerce development team:

1️⃣ A plan before any design
A good team starts with strategy. You should see the scope, the priorities and the order of work before anyone designs a page or writes code. That way, surprises show up on paper, not after launch.

2️⃣ A platform chosen for fit
The right platform depends on your catalog, your growth plans and how you sell. Virtina scores every project on 20 parameters and recommends what fits. It works across 10+ platforms, so the advice isn't about habit.

3️⃣ A build shaped around how you sell
A wholesale store needs different features than a shop selling to shoppers. Tiered pricing, bulk orders and quote requests matter to one. Subscriptions and cart reminders matter to the other. Marketplaces and multi-store setups have their own needs. Good teams build what your model needs and skip the rest.

4️⃣ Support after the launch
Launch day isn't the finish line. A dedicated project manager keeps you updated at every milestone. A 30-day warranty covers issues found after release. The work is held to an ISO 9001:2015 quality standard.

Teams that plan first, pick the platform on fit and stay after launch are easier to work with and easier to trust.
