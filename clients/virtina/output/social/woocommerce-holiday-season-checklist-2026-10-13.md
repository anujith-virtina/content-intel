## LinkedIn

🚨 Is your WooCommerce store ready for the holiday rush?

Adobe forecasts a record $275.1 billion in US online holiday sales, up 6.7%. Black Friday lands on November 27.

Here are 5 checks to run before the traffic arrives:

1️⃣ Site speed
Test your home, category, product, cart and checkout pages on mobile, then fix the slowest one first.

2️⃣ Checkout
Place real test orders on phone and desktop, and confirm every gateway, coupon and email works.

3️⃣ Stock and shipping
Keep stock counts accurate across channels and publish last-order dates you can actually meet.

4️⃣ Order handling
Walk one order through alerts, fulfillment and support so nothing relies on manual work.

5️⃣ Live monitoring
Watch uptime, conversion rate, failed payments and stock on top sellers while the sale runs.

💡 Most holiday failures are predictable, so you can test each one this week.
