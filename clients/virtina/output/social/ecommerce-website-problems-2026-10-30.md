## LinkedIn

Your shoppers will never tell you your checkout is broken. They just leave.

Halloween lands on a Saturday this year. Extra traffic hits the same checkout, product pages and payment setup as always. That is when hidden problems cost the most.

Here are 5 nightmares worth checking before the weekend:

1️⃣ Checkout won't finish
The button spins and nothing happens, or a valid address gets rejected. Every failure here costs a sale from someone who had already decided to buy. Your own test orders work because your address sits inside your shipping zone. Test as a guest with three different addresses.

2️⃣ Slow pages
Grey boxes where products should be. Images popping in one at a time. A filter that lags after a tap. A shopper with other tabs open will not wait to find out. Check your best-selling product page, not only the home page.

3️⃣ A store that feels bad on a phone
Responsive only means the layout fits the screen. Can someone actually buy with one thumb? Tiny buttons, stacked pop-ups and a cookie bar covering the page push them away. Buy something on a real phone and time it.

4️⃣ Payment failures that are not declines
A real decline belongs to one card or one customer. A technical failure hits many customers at once, often right after a change. Worst case, the customer is charged and the order shows as failed. Check your failed orders for the same error repeating.

5️⃣ Stock and orders that disagree
When your store and warehouse hold different numbers, shoppers buy items you don't have. Then come the apology email, the refund and a customer who may not return. Compare your ten best sellers across every system.

Fifteen minutes now is cheaper than a lost weekend of sales. Pick one check per nightmare and do it on your phone before the traffic arrives.
