## LinkedIn

🚨 Peptide store limited on paid ads? You still need a way to be found.

Organic search follows different rules from paid ads, but the law still applies to every page you publish. Your own product claims are the biggest risk.

Here are 4 steps to take before you invest in peptide SEO:

1️⃣ Verify what you may sell
Confirm each product's status and your target countries before you build content around it.

2️⃣ Don't rely on the label
A "research use only" label does not settle intended use, because regulators read your whole website.

3️⃣ Write accurate pages
Write original product and category pages with verified specifications and no unsupported claims.

4️⃣ Fix the basics and measure
Fix indexing and duplicate URLs, then track results in Google Search Console.

💡 Organic search is not a loophole, and nothing guarantees rankings or AI citations. Accurate, crawlable pages give you the best reasonable chance. This is not legal advice.
