## LinkedIn

Your store is legal, your paperwork is ready, and a payment processor still said no.

That is the daily reality for many firearms and ammo sellers online. One provider approves you. Another refuses the same paperwork. Or you launch, then the account closes after launch.

Here are 4 things that keep your orders flowing:

1️⃣ Know why you got a no
Many processors see this category as risky because of regulation, reputation and chargebacks. Some banks refuse it outright. A no often reflects the provider's policy, not the quality of your store.

2️⃣ Make your site easy to trust
Underwriters look at your site before they decide. They want real contact details, clear refund and shipping terms, and a visible age check. Send your licenses, bank statements and a plain description of how an order reaches the dealer together.

3️⃣ Keep disputes low
Most disputes come from confusion, not fraud. A buyer who doesn't know why the order goes to a dealer, or doesn't recognize the name on the statement, may call the bank. Explain each step at checkout and aim to stay under one percent.

4️⃣ Get a second gateway before you need one
Even stable stores lose accounts when a bank changes its policy. A second approved gateway keeps you selling while you sort it out. Approval can take weeks, so set it up early.

If your account is frozen anyway, ask for the reason in writing and reply fast. Don't apply to many processors at once.
