## LinkedIn

Your products can move to BigCommerce. Your rankings, logins and look won't move on their own.

Moving from Volusion to BigCommerce is a realistic switch for most stores. Products, customers and orders all travel with you. The trouble sits in a few things that never move on their own. Those decide whether launch week goes smoothly or turns into a scramble.

Here are 3 things to plan before you switch:

1️⃣ Your old web addresses
Volusion product and category addresses look different from BigCommerce ones. Every page that ranks needs a 301 redirect to its new home. Skip that step and shoppers land on dead pages while Google loses track of what you sell. Match each old address to the closest new page, starting with the ones that earn the most traffic. Rankings often wobble for a few weeks after any platform change. A clean map keeps that wobble small.

2️⃣ Your customers' passwords
Passwords can't be carried over. Your returning customers will try to log in and get turned away. Send a friendly email on launch day that explains the new store and links to a reset page. For wholesale buyers who reorder often, a personal note from your sales team works better. It's easy to handle if you plan for it and painful if you don't.

3️⃣ Your theme and apps
Your design can't be converted. It gets rebuilt on a new theme, so keep what shoppers like and fix what slowed them down. Think of it as a chance to modernize, not a chore. Every tool that touches your store also needs reconnecting, from shipping to accounting to email. Decide on each one early, because these are the most common reason a launch date slips.

None of this is hard if you plan it. Place real test orders before you cut over. Test each shipping method, a coupon and a failed card. Keep your Volusion store live until the new one proves itself.
