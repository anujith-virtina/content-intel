## LinkedIn

Someone told you to switch your store to faster order storage. What if an old plugin breaks your orders?

WooCommerce says the new order storage can create orders up to 5x faster. It says lookups can be up to 40x faster. It also says only plugins that declare support can use it.

The catch is an old plugin you forgot you had. Here are 4 things to check before you switch:

1️⃣ Let your store flag the obvious ones
WooCommerce keeps a list of plugins whose developers have not declared support. You can find it in under a minute. While one of them is active, the switch stays greyed out, so you can't do it by accident. Your store keeps running as it does today.

2️⃣ Look past a clean list
The list only covers plugins that declare support. It can't see your custom code snippets, your accounting connector or a shipping tracker. These can fail quietly, with no error message at all.

3️⃣ Test on a copy, never the live store
Ask your host or developer for a staging copy with your real orders. Place a test order with every payment method you offer. Refund one order in full and another in part. Run your usual sales report. Then repeat it all with the order sync switched off.

4️⃣ Know what a quiet failure looks like
Recent orders go missing from your orders screen. Reports come back empty. Order statuses flip back after you change them. Custom fields go blank in customer emails. Any one of these means stop and fix it first.

You are not forced to switch. If a payment, shipping or subscription plugin you rely on isn't ready, staying on the old storage is a sound choice until it is.
