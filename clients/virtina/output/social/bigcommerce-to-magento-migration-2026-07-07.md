## LinkedIn (long post format)

BigCommerce added a new fee on June 1, 2026.

Every B2B merchant processing purchase orders outside BigCommerce's embedded payment list now pays 0.6% to 2% per transaction. That fee did not exist six months ago.

For a store doing $500K GMV on the Scale plan, that is $3,000 per year in new transaction fees on top of a $3,588 annual subscription. Combined: $6,588 in pure platform fees before a single extension or developer hour.

That math is pushing B2B manufacturers, distributors, and wholesalers to look at Magento.

But here is the honest answer: migration is not the right call for every store.

If your GMV is under $500K, Magento's Year 1 total cost of ownership typically runs $30,000-$60,000. The payback math is negative. Stay on BigCommerce and revisit this when the numbers change.

If you are above $500K and hitting customization ceilings, API limits, or ERP integration constraints that BigCommerce cannot solve, the migration conversation is worth having seriously.

- The honest GMV threshold where migration makes sense
- The 7 phases of a BigCommerce to Magento migration
- The 4 data types that cannot be migrated automatically (and what to do about them)
- How to protect your SEO and AI citation rankings through the cutover
- Real cost and timeline ranges by store size

No pitch. The guide includes a full "when NOT to migrate" section because trust matters more than a closed deal.
