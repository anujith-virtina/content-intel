# Competitor analysis — "Ecommerce redesign vs. website rebuild"

## Searches run

1. `ecommerce website redesign vs rebuild difference`
2. `website redesign vs replatform decision guide`

Both returned real, varied SERPs (no empty/thin result sets). 5 pages fetched in full below; a 6th
candidate (lowcode.agency) 404'd at fetch time and was dropped rather than guessed at.

- Est. word count: ~2,800
- Structure: refresh vs. redesign framing (not rebuild vs. redesign — title doesn't match content focus),

  1. No ecommerce specifics at all — never mentions platform migration, Shopify, WooCommerce, or

- How Virtina's post outperforms: B2B/B2C ecommerce audience specificity throughout, named platforms

## Pattern across all 5 competitors

Every page picks ONE of these approaches but never both:
- A scoring/diagnostic framework (webhelpagency, techtic) **without** a real cost/timeline/risk table, or
- A cost/timeline comparison (cmsminds, paveddigital) **without** ecommerce-specific criteria or process
  detail

None of the five fetched pages:
- Names ecommerce platforms by name (WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Shopify) as part of the decision
  criteria
- Breaks out "what a redesign project actually involves" vs. "what a rebuild/replatform project actually
  involves" as a dedicated process section with phases
- Combines a genuine comparison table AND a named-criteria framework AND ecommerce-specific process detail
  in one article
