## Per-page analysis

### 1. Shopify Enterprise, "B2B Marketplaces: 7 Options for Sellers in 2026"
- Position (tool order, query 1 and 2): appears in both result sets.
- URL: https://www.shopify.com/enterprise/blog/largest-b2b-marketplaces
- Domain: Shopify (platform vendor).
- Published Dec 6, 2025. About 2,500 words (estimate).
- Entries: Faire, Amazon Business, Tundra, Alibaba, Ankorstore, Novi, Abound. Includes pros/cons sections and a fee line per entry.
- Weaknesses:
  1. No ranking method. The order is unexplained. The intro mentions "ten" and "nine" options while the title says seven.
  2. Stale and partly unsourced data in a Dec 2025 post: 2021-2023 market figures and forecasts, and some claims with no source.
  3. Seller-only angle that ends in selling Shopify. No traffic or scale evidence, no mention of sourcing directories (ThomasNet, Made-in-China, IndiaMART, Global Sources).
- How the Virtina post wins: states a disclosed metric and its limits, dates every figure, covers buyer and seller sides, and gives a neutral own-store decision.

### 2. Geekflare, "9 Best B2B Wholesale Marketplaces to Grow Your eCom Business"
- Position: first result for query 2.
- URL: https://geekflare.com/guide/best-b2b-wholesale-marketplaces/
- Domain: Geekflare (affiliate publisher).
- Last updated Apr 30, 2025. About 1,800 words.
- Entries: Alibaba, Handshake, Faire, Amazon Business, Ankorstore, Global Sources, DHgate, Droply by Abound, Tundra.
- Weaknesses:
  1. No stated criteria and no consistent comparison fields. Fees appear for some entries only.
  2. Buyer and brand counts are given with no sources, and no limitations are discussed for any platform.
  3. Affiliate-tracked links are only partly disclosed (a general commission note).
- Virtina wins: same fields for every entry, every figure labelled by source type, one honest limitation per entry.

### 3. CS-Cart, "Top B2B Wholesale Marketplaces: Best Practices to Succeed"
- Position: tool order shows it mid-list for query 2.
- URL: https://www.cs-cart.com/blog/top-b2b-wholesale-marketplaces/
- Domain: CS-Cart (marketplace software vendor, i.e. closest to Virtina's 36161 cluster).
- Jan 27, 2025. About 4,000 words, 18-minute read.
- Entries: Alibaba, Amazon Business, DHgate, Global Sources, Made-in-China, eWorldTrade, IndiaMART, Faire, TradeWheel, Ankorstore.
- Weaknesses:
  1. Claims a ranking from "quantitative metrics" but never shows weights or numbers. Headline figures are uncited. One market-size stat is attributed.
  2. Internal contradictions (for example eWorldTrade user counts differ between sections, TradeWheel supplier counts differ).
  3. Heavy CS-Cart promotion and anonymised case studies with no measurable result. Half the page is about building a marketplace.
- Virtina wins: one stated metric, applied to every entry, with a date. No contradictions because each figure has one source label.

- URL: https://ipsnews.net/business/2026/08/01/top-10-best-b2b-marketplaces-in-usa-2026-trusted-platforms-for-wholesale-buyers-suppliers/

## What the SERP gets wrong (the gap)

- 0 of 5 states a ranking metric that a reader can check. The only "quantitative" claim (CS-Cart) is unsupported.
- 0 of 5 dates its figures against a single point in time. Most mix 2020-2025 numbers.
- 3 of 5 contain unsourced statistics. 2 of 5 are promoting the author's own product.
- 0 of 5 helps a manufacturer decide between a marketplace and its own B2B store. Shopify's piece ends in a Shopify pitch.
- None separates open marketplaces from sourcing directories (ThomasNet, Made-in-China, IndiaMART, Global Sources work by inquiry or RFQ, not checkout). Reader mislabels them.
- Glossary pages (Oro, G2, elogic, Sharetribe, Dokan) fill the "b2b marketplaces" head term. A ranked, dated, metric-based list is open space.
