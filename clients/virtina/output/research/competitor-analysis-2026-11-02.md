## Pages fetched (4 full fetches plus 1 blocked)

### 1. Result order #1 in query 2
- **URL:** https://channeldock.com/en/blogs/b2b-quick-order-form-wholesale/
- **Title:** B2B Quick Order Forms: The Wholesale Shortcut That Still Needs Control
- **Domain:** ChannelDock (Dutch order-management SaaS vendor)
- **Est. words:** about 1,800. No visible date; text references 2026.
- **Structure:** why quick order is a portal requirement, three entry patterns, validation flow ("rough SKU list to warehouse-ready order"), line-level error messages, basic vs controlled flow, measurement, FAQ.
- **Weaknesses:**
  1. Platform blindness: names Adobe Commerce, BigCommerce, OroCommerce and "Shopify B2B apps" in one list sentence. WooCommerce is absent. No how-to on any platform.
  2. Statistics are secondhand: a Gartner buyer-preference figure and a "one-third of B2B online orders contain errors" claim relayed via Digital Commerce 360 and Sana. Neither is verified at the primary. Its "3 modes / 7 checks / 1 queue" counts are the author's own framing.
  3. Contract pricing, ERP hand-off and punchout are not worked through: ERP appears once in passing, punchout never, mobile never. It sells its own order-intake product at the close.
- **Virtina outperforms by:** a per-platform native-vs-plugin table with primary links, the contract-price and stock-per-line check, ERP/punchout hand-off, no unsourced stats.

### 3. Result order #9 in query 1 (also query 2)

## Gap summary (what none of the five do)

1. No page compares WooCommerce, Adobe Commerce, BigCommerce B2B Edition and Shopify side by side.
2. No page treats the partial-add question: when 3 of 40 pasted lines are bad, does the form block or add the 37 good lines?
3. No page ties per-line contract price and per-line availability to the ERP as source of truth.
4. No page distinguishes quick order form, reorder list and punchout by buyer type.
5. Only one source (XICTRON) addresses mobile or barcode entry. None cites a primary source for any adoption statistic.

## Verdict on competitor strength (honesty rule)

Competition is weak on depth, strong on domain authority for vendors. Winning requires the platform table and the line-level specificity, not word count. The best single competitor is XICTRON on structure (but Shopware only) and ChannelDock on validation framing (but with no platform detail). No competitor is stronger than expected.
