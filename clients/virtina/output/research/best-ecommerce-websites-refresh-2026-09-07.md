## 2. THE RANKED DATA

### 2.1 Metric decision

**Rank by ONE metric: total monthly website visits, Semrush Traffic Analytics, July 2026 (data last updated 2026-08-12).**

Why Semrush and not Similarweb, given Similarweb is the SERP norm:
- Similarweb.com was **unreachable from this environment** (connection refused on every attempt, including the top-websites category pages and individual domain pages). No Similarweb figure in this file was read from Similarweb directly. I will not restate second-hand Similarweb numbers as if I verified them.
- Semrush Traffic Analytics is a named, dated, independently checkable panel of the same class. Each figure below was read from that company's own domain page, one domain at a time, on the same metric and the same month.
- Wikipedia's "List of most-visited websites" uses Similarweb **and** Semrush side by side as its two reference sources, which supports treating Semrush as an acceptable primary of the same standing.

Rules applied so the ranking is honest:
- **One flagship domain per company.** Amazon's country domains are listed separately by Semrush; amazon.de alone had 435.13M visits in July 2026. They are reported as a footnote, not as separate ranks.
- **Shopping destinations only.** Excluded: shop.app (Shopify's app, 317.7M) and shopify.com because they are commerce software, not shopping sites; samsung.com (407.2M) and apple.com because they are single-brand manufacturer stores, not shopping destinations; doordash.com because it is food delivery.
- **No blending.** App-user counts are never mixed into the visit ranking. They appear only in section 2.3, clearly separated.

**This is the gap in the SERP.** Not one competing page explains why the biggest sellers are missing from the traffic rankings they publish. Several make it worse by blending app users into a visits list (Proxidize puts Shopee in at "393M monthly app users" beside Amazon's "2.7B monthly visits", which is not a comparison).
