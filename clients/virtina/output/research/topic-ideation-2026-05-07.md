## CANDIDATE 1 — RECOMMENDED #1

**Working title:** How to connect WooCommerce to your ERP: a practical guide for B2B manufacturers and distributors

**Primary keyword:** WooCommerce ERP integration

**Search intent:** Commercial / transactional (decision-stage buyers evaluating integration options or agencies)

**Estimated monthly search volume:** Medium (500–5k) — "WooCommerce ERP integration" and variants like "WooCommerce SAP integration", "WooCommerce NetSuite connector" each pull meaningful volume

**Why this fits Virtina:**
- Virtina is a certified WooCommerce Expert with deep B2B manufacturing/distribution experience — this is their exact wheelhouse
- Their existing Magento ERP integration post (slug: magento-erp-integration, published Nov 2024) proves the format works; this is the WooCommerce twin that is missing
- Target reader is a VP eCommerce or Head of IT at a manufacturer running WooCommerce who needs to sync orders, inventory, and pricing with SAP, NetSuite, Dynamics 365, or a custom ERP — a high-value buyer ready to hire
- Virtina's service offer (integration development, custom plugin dev, ERP connectors) maps directly to the conversion action

**Why this is original:**
No Virtina post specifically addresses WooCommerce ERP integration for B2B. The Magento ERP post (ID 37600) covers Magento only. The WooCommerce inventory management post (ID 36598) is a plugin list, not an integration guide. Zero overlap.

**Strongest angle / contrarian thesis:**
Most WooCommerce ERP integration articles sell you on a connector plugin and stop there. The real problem is data mapping — your ERP's item master rarely matches WooCommerce's product schema, and that mismatch is what breaks B2B pricing, inventory sync, and order routing. This article argues that choosing a connector without a data mapping plan first is the single most common — and most expensive — integration mistake manufacturers make.

**Estimated word count:** 2,000–2,500 words (standard guide format, fits MUST-FOLLOW-RULES.md structure perfectly)

**Scores:**
- Originality: 9/10 (Magento ERP post exists but WooCommerce B2B ERP is uncovered)
- Audience fit: 10/10 (directly addresses VP eCommerce at manufacturer/distributor running WooCommerce)
- SEO opportunity: 8/10 (medium volume, manageable competition, high commercial intent)
- Brand alignment: 10/10 (WooCommerce Expert credential + integration service + B2B manufacturing focus)
- **Total: 37/40**
