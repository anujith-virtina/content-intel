# Fact sheet: citable facts for the "leaving Shopify" post

All facts checked 2026-07-24. **Reminder: MUST-FOLLOW-RULES.md section 6 bans linking to shopify.com or any competitor domain, and caps external links at 2 total.** Several facts below are verifiable but cannot be hyperlinked to their primary source (shopify.com) — flagged explicitly.

## 1. Shopify plan pricing (2026)

**Claim:** Shopify's core paid plans are Basic ($39/month), Grow ($105/month), and Advanced ($399/month) on monthly billing, dropping to roughly $29/$79/$299 per month on annual billing (a ~25% discount).

**Source:** Cross-referenced across multiple independent pricing-tracker sites (websitebuilderexpert.com, demandsage.com, craftshift.com) which report consistent figures. A direct fetch of shopify.com/pricing returned India-region pricing (INR), confirming the page is geo-targeted but not contradicting the USD figures reported elsewhere.

**Verdict:** VERIFIED (via consistent third-party reporting), but the primary source (shopify.com) **cannot be linked** per section 6. Use as an unlinked, paraphrased fact ("Shopify's published plans run $39 to $399 a month before annual discounts") or attribute generically ("Shopify's own pricing page").

## 2. Shopify transaction fees when not using Shopify Payments

**Claim:** Merchants who use a third-party payment gateway instead of Shopify Payments pay an additional transaction fee on top of gateway fees: 2% on the Basic plan, 1% on Grow, and 0.6% on Advanced.

**Source:** Same cross-referenced pricing-tracker cluster as above (websitebuilderexpert.com, demandsage.com).

**Verdict:** VERIFIED via consistent third-party reporting. Same linking restriction as #1 — cannot cite shopify.com directly; usable as an unlinked fact.

## 3. Shopify's Terms of Service permit account termination without notice

**Claim:** Shopify's general Terms of Service state that Shopify may modify or terminate the service, the terms, or a merchant's account "for any reason, without notice at any time" (subject to applicable law).

**Source:** Directly confirmed via search of shopify.com/legal/terms content. No independent secondary source with sufficient authority was found quoting this clause verbatim for citation purposes — most secondary discussion lives in low-authority merchant-support blogs or Shopify's own community forum, neither citable per Virtina's source-quality bar.

**Verdict:** VERIFIED (primary source language), but **shopify.com cannot be linked** under section 6, and no adequate secondary/independent source exists to link instead. **Recommendation: use this as a paraphrased, unlinked fact only** ("Shopify's own terms of service allow it to end your account for any reason, without notice"). Do not present this as a quoted/linked citation.
