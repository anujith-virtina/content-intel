## H2 #1: What happened to vape stores on Shopify (id: what-happened)

Shopify told ENDS merchants in late June 2026 to remove every vape product from their stores within about two weeks or risk suspension. Individual merchant notices went out around June 24, with a compliance deadline of July 7-8, 2026. Reuters first reported the policy on June 23, 2026, before most merchants had seen their own notice.

The policy covers every ENDS product, including e-cigarettes, e-liquids, vaporizers, and parts, regardless of nicotine content or FDA authorization status. Shopify didn't ask merchants to remove only unauthorized products, it removed the category outright. The trigger was a November 2025 [EXTERNAL LINK: state attorneys general letter](https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/co-leading-bipartisan-coalition-attorney-general-bonta-calls-shopify-crack-down). A coalition of 25 states plus DC, Puerto Rico, and New York City sent it.

A merchant with a clean compliance record got the same notice as one selling gray-market disposables. There was no manual review path published, only a deadline.

[H3: Why did Shopify ban ENDS products]

Shopify's policy traces to the PACT Act (Prevent All Cigarette Trafficking Act), extended to cover ENDS products in 2021. Sellers must meet [EXTERNAL LINK: PACT Act requirements](https://www.atf.gov/alcohol-tobacco/prevent-all-cigarette-trafficking-pact-act), including ATF and state registration, monthly sales reporting, and adult-signature delivery. Layered on top is the FDA's Premarket Tobacco Product Application (PMTA) process.

Only a small fraction of ENDS products nationally hold that FDA marketing authorization. Shopify's ban ignored authorization status entirely. That's what turned a compliance sweep into a blanket, platform-wide removal.

[H3: What this means for you]

If you sold ENDS products on Shopify, your store was at risk of suspension regardless of your compliance status. The policy was categorical, not case by case. FDA authorization didn't exempt you, and Shopify published no manual appeal path.

- **No published appeal path.** Shopify gave merchants a deadline, not a review process.
- **FDA authorization didn't matter.** Fully authorized products were removed along with everything else.
- **Waiting past the deadline was risky.** Continued listings put the whole store at risk, not just the flagged products.
