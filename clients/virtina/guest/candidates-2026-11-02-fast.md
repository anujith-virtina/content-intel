## Notes on top picks

- Callbox is a B2B lead-generation firm. Posts carry no author names on its blog index. Link type is not stated. Quality 3, not higher.
- WebAppick describes the program as "guest article contribution exchanges". Treat that as a possible link-swap signal. Do not offer any exchange. Ask in the pitch that it is plain editorial.
- API2Cart requires a closing section about API2Cart. That is promotional copy for the host and conflicts with the "stand alone" rule. Write it neutrally or skip.
- Locad wants a company email and 2,000+ words. Strong stated speed. Undated blog.
- GemPages returned HTTP 429 on four tries. Its "2 working days" figure comes from a search result snippet only.

## Owned-profile options (not guest posts, not tracked as guest rows)

Not fetched in this run. Verify before use.

1. Medium publications. Medium's help center says open-to-all publications cap new writers at 3 active submissions. Pick a publication with real recent posts. Links in Medium are generally nofollow.
2. LinkedIn articles or newsletter on the Virtina company page. Instant, no review.
3. DEV or Hashnode. Only suits developer-angle WooCommerce posts.
