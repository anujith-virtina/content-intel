1. Cloudways. DR 91 (BP). Last blog post Oct 16 2026. Nofollow x2. Free, not stated as paid. WordPress, WooCommerce, speed, Core Web Vitals. 1,500+ words. Outline first, then form. Needs one link to a Cloudways post. Best fit.
2. Convert. DR 77 (BP). Recent posts Oct 12 to 21 2026. One dofollow link. Free. CRO how-tos. Google Form. Very selective.
3. eCommerce Fastlane. DR estimate, unverified. Updated content Oct 29 2026. Guest posts "free of charge, non-sponsored". Avoid its paid link insertions. 1,000 to 2,000 words. Contact form.
4. Elogic. DR estimate, unverified. Posts Oct 29 2026. Unpaid, one nofollow link. 1,500+ words. Magento and ecommerce tech.
5. Ecommerce CEO. DR estimate, unverified. Latest post Sept 7 2026. One dofollow bio link. 1,500+ words. Sponsored option is separate, do not use.
6. Search Engine Journal. DR 91 (BP list quoted by lilachbullock.com). Posts Oct 30 2026. Application form. No promotional links. Cost not stated, confirm unpaid before accepting.
7. HubSpot Blog. DR 93 (BP). Pitch only, no guaranteed placement. One body link. Cost not stated.
8. Maker's Row. Estimate, unverified. B2B manufacturing fit. Posts undated. Low confidence.

## Rejected

- WP Mayor: paid placements $250 to $750.
- Shopify Partner and Shopify blog: paid program, not accepting unsolicited pitches.
- Practical Ecommerce: no guest posts, paid staff writers.
- AllBusiness: $1,000 administrative fee.
- Omniconvert: partners only.
- API2Cart: requires a promotional section about itself plus reciprocal mention.
- E-Commerce Nation: newest articles show 2024 image paths, archive newest month July 2026, no 60-day evidence.
- Lilach Bullock: sponsored posts offered, blog is templated mass content, low quality.
- Mention, Taggbox, SurveySparrow: off-topic.
- Business 2 Community, MarketingProfs, MarketingTech, ResellerClub, Oro: no guidelines confirmed (403, or no contributor terms) or vendor-promotional.
- BSS Commerce, WebAppick (2,500 words, 3 past guest links): not confirmed free, vendor blogs.
