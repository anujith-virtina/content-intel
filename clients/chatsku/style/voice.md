# Voice — ChatSKU

## Tone
Direct, confident, slightly cheeky. Anti-corporate, anti-jargon. Punchy short sentences mixed with explanatory ones. Often opens with a vivid scenario or contrarian statement. Comfortable with sales tension.

## Examples from the live site
- "Your catalog should be selling. Even when you're not."
- "Buyers call at 8pm. Nobody's there. By morning, they bought from your competitor."
- "Think of ChatSKU as your night-shift sales rep."
- "Most suppliers will lose to competitors who respond faster. Don't be one of them."
- "It never quits, never takes PTO, and never forgets a SKU."

## Person
Second person ("you", "your") most of the time. First person plural ("we") when speaking as the brand.

## Sentence structure
Vary length aggressively: 3-word punches followed by 15-word explanations. Lead with the strongest sentence in each section.

## Punctuation
- Em dashes BANNED — replace with periods, commas, hyphens
- Semicolons avoided
- Oxford comma yes
- Exclamation marks rare
- Italics for emphasis on key phrases (matching homepage's *night-shift sales rep* style)

## Banned words and phrases

AI tells (never use): delve, leverage, navigate (verb), realm, landscape, ecosystem, "in today's fast-paced world", "it's important to note", "in conclusion", "innovative solutions"

Hype: revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, world-class, best-in-class, "transform your..."

ChatSKU-specific:
- Never describe ChatSKU as "just a chatbot" — always position above generic chatbots
- Never say "AI-powered" as generic modifier — be specific about what the AI does
- Never say "solutions" as filler
- Never say "chatbot" alone — use "AI catalog assistant" or stronger framing

## Vocabulary
Always say:
- "catalog" (not "product database")
- "buyer" (not generic "user")
- "quote" (not "estimate")
- "answers" (the core value prop)
- "after-hours" (not "after business hours")
- "AI catalog assistant" (not "chatbot" alone)

Never say:
- "chatbot" alone
- "AI-powered" as filler
- "solutions" as noun filler
- "Schedule a demo to learn more" (lead with self-serve demo + free trial)

## Formatting
Sentence case headings, never Title Case. Bold for genuine emphasis only. No emoji in blog body.

## Opening hooks — reference patterns
- Time-of-day scenario: "It's 9pm. A buyer finds your catalog online..."
- Loss scenario: "Last quarter, you lost 17 deals you didn't know about..."
- Vivid friction: "Your catalog has 5,000 SKUs. Your buyer asks one question. Nobody's there to answer it."
- Contrarian flip: "Everyone's talking about AI. Most B2B manufacturers are doing it wrong."

## Section transitions
Short and punchy. Single-sentence bridges work well: "Here's the part nobody tells you." / "But there's a catch." / "The fix isn't complicated."

## Close
Always with a concrete CTA — never abstract "learn more" language.
- "Start a free trial — no credit card, live in hours."
- "See how ChatSKU captures the buyers your team misses."
