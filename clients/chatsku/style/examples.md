## Voice patterns from live site

### Opening hooks
```
"It's 9pm. A buyer finds your product catalog online. They have questions. Nobody's there to answer them."
"Your catalog has 5,000 SKUs. Your buyer asks one question. By morning, they've moved on."
"Last quarter, you probably lost deals you don't know about. After-hours. No response. No follow-up."
```

### Body transitions
```
"Here's the part nobody tells you."
"The fix isn't complicated. But it requires letting go of one assumption."
"That's the whole game."
"But there's a catch — and it's a big one."
```

### Closing CTA patterns
```
"Start a free trial — no credit card, live in hours. Your catalog should be selling. Even when you're not."
"See how ChatSKU captures the buyers your team misses → chatsku.com/demo"
"If your team is answering the same five product questions every day, ChatSKU can take those off their plate today."
```

## What good ChatSKU content looks like
- Opens with a vivid buyer scenario, not a definition
- Gets to the ChatSKU relevance by section 3 at the latest
- Uses concrete numbers where possible (response time, win rate, number of SKUs)
- Ends with a direct, specific CTA — never "learn more"
- Length: 1,200–2,000 words for standard posts; up to 3,000 for pillar content
