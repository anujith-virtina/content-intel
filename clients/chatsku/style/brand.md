# Brand — ChatSKU

## Positioning
ChatSKU turns existing B2B catalogs (PDFs, Excel, ERP exports) into 24/7 AI catalog assistants that answer buyer questions, build quotes, and capture leads — without rebuilding the website. One line of code, live in under a day. For manufacturers, distributors, and wholesalers.

Tagline themes:
- "night-shift sales rep"
- "your catalog should be selling, even when you're not"
- "your catalog, selling 24/7"

## Single product: ChatSKU AI catalog assistant
- 1-line script tag deployment
- Customer groups and tiered pricing built in
- Quote/RFQ workflows
- Multi-source catalog ingestion (PDF, Excel, ERP, CSV)
- CRM/ecommerce/ERP sync
- Real-time analytics on buyer intent

## Things never to say
- "Just a chatbot" — contradicts positioning above generic chatbots
- "Replaces your sales team" — ChatSKU augments, never replaces
- "AI-powered" as generic filler — be specific about what the AI does
- "Schedule a demo to learn more" — lead with self-serve demo and free trial
- "Innovative platform" or "industry-leading" — meaningless hype

## Competitors (do not cite or link)
- Generic chatbots: Drift, Intercom, Tidio, LiveChat, Zendesk Chat
- B2B platforms with catalog features: BigCommerce B2B Edition, Adobe Commerce
- Other AI catalog tools (do not name or link)

## Acceptable to mention factually
ERPs: NetSuite, SAP, Acumatica, Sage, Epicor, Dynamics 365
eCommerce platforms: Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento (Virtina builds these — OK to reference)
CRM: HubSpot, Salesforce, Klaviyo
Quoting tools: generic references OK (not competitor tools)

## Themes ChatSKU owns
1. After-hours buyer capture (the "8pm buyer" scenario)
2. Quote workflow automation for B2B
3. Customer group / tiered pricing complexity
4. Catalog-to-chat without site rebuilds
5. ROI math on lost B2B leads

## Themes to avoid
- Generic ecommerce platform comparisons not tied to B2B catalog/quote workflows
- General AI think pieces with no ChatSKU-specific angle
- Consumer ecommerce topics
