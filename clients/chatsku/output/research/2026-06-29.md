## 3. Verified stats & citations (2024–2026)

- **WooCommerce market share**: ~33.4% of global ecommerce stores by store count; ~4.5M live stores (StoreLeads, 2025–2026). W3Techs: used by ~8.7% of all websites (Jan 2026). Sources: [redstagfulfillment](https://redstagfulfillment.com/what-is-woocommerces-market-share/), [storeleads](https://storeleads.app/reports/woocommerce).
- **Rep-free buying**: 67% of B2B buyers prefer a rep-free experience for at least part of their purchase (Gartner press release, 2026-03-09). 70% prefer a fully digital self-service experience. Source: [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-03-09-gartner-sales-survey-finds-67-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-experience).
- **AI in buying**: 89% of B2B buyers use generative AI for self-guided research; 86% of B2B purchases stall (Forrester). 45% used AI during a recent purchase (Gartner).
- **WooCommerce B2B plugins** (factual landscape):
  - **B2BKing** — full dynamic rule engine, customer groups, unique per-customer prices, built-in RFQ/quotes module. Source: [wordpress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/b2bking-wholesale-for-woocommerce/).
  - **Wholesale Suite** — wholesale role + per-product price field; lighter quote logic. Source: [wordpress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/woocommerce-wholesale-prices/).
  - **B2B for WooCommerce** — customer groups, tiered pricing, request-a-quote.
- **WooCommerce technical**: WooCommerce 8.x/9.x lines; product CSV importer/exporter is built in ([woocommerce.com docs](https://woocommerce.com/document/product-csv-importer-exporter/)); HPOS (High-Performance Order Storage) default since 8.2; PHP 7.4+ minimum.

## 5. Worked ROI example (illustrative)

- Before: 1.6% conversion → 16 orders → $11,760/mo.

- Gain: $13,230/mo = **$158,760/yr**. Math verified internally consistent.
