## 2. Verified Adobe Commerce B2B facts (2025–2026)

Adobe Commerce ships native B2B features (Magento Open Source does not; needs extensions):
- **Company accounts + hierarchies** — roles: admin, requester, approver, finance reviewer; mapped to procurement policy / cost centers.
- **Shared catalogs** — per-company custom pricing levels across one or multiple websites.
- **Negotiable quotes** — buyer/seller price negotiation initiated from the cart.
- **Requisition lists + quick order** — bulk reorder workflows.
- **Multi-company / multi-website management** — storefront company switcher.
- **APIs**: REST + GraphQL expose catalog, pricing, stock. Version line: Magento/Adobe Commerce 2.4.x (2.4.7/2.4.8 by 2026). Front ends: Luma, Hyva, headless PWA.
- Sources: [Adobe Commerce B2B intro](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/commerce-admin/b2b/introduction), [Atwix Magento B2B features](https://www.atwix.com/magento/magento-b2b-features/).
