## How do the delivery models differ, a phased project or a self-serve widget?

HumCommerce delivers its assistant as a phased project. ChatSKU delivers it as a self-serve widget you can install in under a day. That one difference shapes everything else.

A project means discovery, integration and a pilot before buyers see anything. That effort buys a deep ERP connection. If your answers must come from live systems, the project is the right shape.

A widget means a fast start and fewer moving parts. ERP and PIM integration is available on paid plans.

Think about who does the work. With HumCommerce, an agency team does it with you. With ChatSKU, you start from a catalog file and add one line of code.

Think about what you need first. HumCommerce needs integration access to your systems. ChatSKU needs a catalog and a site that allows custom HTML.

Think about changing your mind. ChatSKU offers a [free trial](https://chatsku.com/signup/) with usage limits. HumCommerce does not mention a trial on the page we checked (2026-11-05). It offers a demo and a free audit.

Cost visibility differs too. ChatSKU publishes its plans on the [ChatSKU pricing page](https://chatsku.com/chatsku-pricing/). HumCommerce's pricing is by contact form.

## How do they handle customer-specific pricing and quotes?

Both apply customer-specific pricing and both capture quote requests, but they pull the price from different places. HumCommerce reads contract terms from your systems. ChatSKU applies customer groups and tiered pricing.

On HumCommerce's page, the assistant applies contract pricing, volume tiers and surcharges to draft quotes. It respects credit limits, approval chains and role-based visibility. Complex deals go to a CPQ tool (checked 2026-11-05).

If your deals run on negotiated contracts and approval chains, that is the stronger fit.

ChatSKU supports customer groups and tiered pricing on paid plans, not in the free trial. Buyers can request quotes in chat. You can set percent or fixed discounts by customer group, and quotes export to CSV.

Picture the 8pm buyer. She wants a price and a quote. With a quote request captured in chat, your team opens it in the morning with the product and quantity attached. Without it, she waits.

Our guide to [RFQ automation](https://chatsku.com/rfq-automation-manufacturers/) shows how that handoff works.

## Who should pick the HumCommerce B2B AI Assistant, and who should pick ChatSKU?

Pick HumCommerce if you run Adobe Commerce, need live ERP answers in chat and can fund a phased rollout. Pick ChatSKU if your catalog lives in PDFs, spreadsheets or a plain site, and you want to be live in under a day with a free trial first.

### Pick HumCommerce when

- You run an Adobe Commerce or Magento store and want the assistant native to it.
- Answers must come live from the ERP, such as credit limits, multi-warehouse stock, order status and approval chains.
- You have an integration budget and room for discovery, pilot and rollout.
- You want an agency to build and manage the store as well as the assistant.
- Complex deals need a CPQ handoff.

### Pick ChatSKU when

- Your catalog sits in PDFs, spreadsheets or CSV files, and you want no platform change.
- You want to go live in under a day with one line of code.
- You want to test on a free trial before you pay.
- Your site runs on WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Shopify or a custom build.
- After-hours buyer capture and quote capture matter more to you than live ERP lookups.

ChatSKU is [built for distributors](https://chatsku.com/for-b2b-manufacturers-distributors-and-wholesalers/), manufacturers and wholesalers. Still unsure? Ask each vendor the same five buyer questions in a demo.

- A spec question your catalog can answer.
- A price for one named customer.
- A stock question for one warehouse.
- A quote request for a real order.
- A question the catalog cannot answer.

A good answer is correct, uses that customer's price, and hands off cleanly. For the last question, a good answer admits the gap and passes the buyer to a person. You can run the same questions in the [live demo](https://chatsku.com/demo/).
