How do I calculate my lost revenue from slow quote response?

This is the core section. The following five steps function as a lost B2B revenue calculator you can run right now with numbers from your CRM and website analytics.

  1. Monthly inbound leads × percentage that arrive after hours = after-hours leads
  2. After-hours leads × percentage that get no same-session response = abandoned leads
  3. Abandoned leads × your average quote value = revenue at risk per month
  4. Revenue at risk × your historical close rate = monthly lost revenue
  5. Monthly lost revenue × 12 = annual lost revenue

The inputs you need are your monthly inbound lead count, your average quote value, and your historical close rate. Your after-hours percentage defaults to 52% if you do not have your own data. Your abandonment rate defaults to 70% if you have no same-session response system in place.

  • 70% get no same-session response = 18 abandoned leads

  • 28% historical close rate
  • Monthly lost revenue: $21,168

How do I audit my current lead response process?

Before you fix the gap, you need to know where it is. Most manufacturers and distributors discover they cannot answer questions 3, 4, and 6 below. That inability is itself the finding.

  • ✓
    Track inbound by time of day. Do you know what percentage of leads arrive outside business hours? If not, you are measuring the wrong thing.
  • ✓
    Measure your average first-response time. Not your best case. Your median. Across all inbound channels.
  • ✓
    Check for same-session acknowledgment. Does every after-hours lead receive an automated, personalized response within 5 minutes? Or do they wait until morning?
  • ✓
    Segment close rates by response speed. Leads responded to in under 5 minutes versus over 1 hour. If you do not have this data, that is the first gap to fix.
  • ✓
    Review lost-deal analysis. Do you ask why you lost? "Went with another vendor" is not an answer. "Vendor responded faster" is.
  • ✓
    Identify catalog questions that go unanswered. What are buyers asking that your team cannot answer in real time? Spec questions, MOQ, lead time, pricing. These are the abandonment triggers.

How do I track after-hours lead loss?

Start with your CRM. Filter leads by time of submission. Compare close rates for business-hours leads versus after-hours leads. The delta is your baseline. Yes. B2B buyers research across multiple sessions and multiple days. But the first vendor to respond to a specific inquiry sets the frame. After-hours B2B abandonment is structurally worse than B2C because the alternative is not "buy it elsewhere online." It is "call your competitor's sales rep tomorrow." The dependency on a human response makes the delay more damaging in B2B than in any self-serve consumer context.
