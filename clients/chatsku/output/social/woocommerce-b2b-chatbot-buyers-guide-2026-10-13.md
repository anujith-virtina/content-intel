## Poster

Vetting a WooCommerce chatbot? / Ask better questions
Test it on your store, not the vendor's

1. Pricing plugin: Demo on your own wholesale login
2. Messy variations: Tier plus variation, tested live
3. Volume spike: Ask what the overage model costs
4. Unknown answers: Guess, escalate, or say so?

**Demo it on your pricing plugin** Ask the vendor to run the demo on your store, logged in as a real wholesale account. A list-price-only demo tells you nothing about your tiers.

**Test your messiest variation** Load your hardest variable product with group tiers. Check that the bot quotes the logged-in group's price, not the public list price.

**Price your busiest month** Ask about the overage model before you sign. Then ask what last month's real traffic would have cost under it.

**Watch what it does when unsure** Ask a question the bot should not answer confidently. A good vendor can explain in one sentence whether it guesses, escalates, or stops.
