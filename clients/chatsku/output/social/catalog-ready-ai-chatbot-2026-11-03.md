A buyer types AX-4410 into your search box.
Your catalog only knows it as AX4410.
No results. She opens a competitor's site.

Nothing was wrong with your product.
Your catalog could not understand her.

This is why adding a chat to a messy catalog does not work.
The chat answers from your catalog.
If the catalog is messy, the answers are messy, only faster.

Try this today.
Take your top five part numbers.
Type each one with a dash, without a dash, and with a space.
Do all three find the same product?

I counted seven checks like this before a catalog is ready for a chat.
The first one takes ten minutes.

How many of your five part numbers passed?
