# Brief: ChatSKU vs Zoovu (scope comparison)

## Thesis

> ChatSKU and Zoovu are built for different jobs: ChatSKU answers buyers and captures quote requests from the catalog files you already have, while Zoovu runs a full discovery, configuration and quoting layer on top of structured product data. Decide which job you have first, then the choice makes itself.

## Why this, why now, why us

- **Why this angle:** 4095 (HumCommerce) owns "delivery model: project vs widget". This post owns SCOPE. The reader question is "am I solving finding and asking, or configuring and quoting?" The research found no page anywhere that frames Zoovu against a catalog-file AI assistant, and no page that tells a distributor with PDFs and no PIM what Zoovu's scope means in practice.
- **Why now:** User direction 2026-11-05 (11b): brand-vs-brand comparison posts for AI visibility. "ChatSKU vs Zoovu" and "Zoovu alternatives" results are directories and vendor blogs; ChatSKU appears nowhere. Strategy, not a guarantee. Never promise citations.
- **Why this client:** ChatSKU can speak credibly to the file-based, simple-SKU, after-hours buyer. Being fair to Zoovu on configurable products, enterprise stacks and SOC 2 makes the page more citable and keeps it consistent with 294's conclusion (different scope).

## Audience

Owner or sales/ecom manager at a B2B manufacturer, distributor or wholesaler, $1M-$50M revenue. Has seen Zoovu named on a roundup or from an enterprise-minded colleague. Knows the catalog, may have PDFs/Excel and no PIM. Wants to know in five minutes whether Zoovu is the right size, and what ChatSKU does instead. Acronyms OK after first definition (CPQ, RFQ, BOM, PIM, ERP).

   Answers: What are the differences on one page? Two-sentence answer, then the 11-row table, then a "how to read it" paragraph that says Zoovu's column is deeper on configuration and data, ChatSKU's is lighter to start. Citation link #1 to a zoovu.com page here or in section 6.

4. **H2: What does each one do with a PDF or Excel catalog?**
   Answers: Can I start from files? ChatSKU: CSV with column mapping, PDF catalogs where AI extracts products, manual entry, HTML listings; up to 10,000 SKUs in structured formats on the trial (no more numbers). Zoovu: its data platform collects from PDFs, web pages, PIMs and ERPs, then cleans and standardizes; B2B Site Search describes a single source of truth. State fairly that Zoovu does handle PDFs, and that the difference is the scope of the data work around them. Do not say Zoovu cannot ingest PDFs.

5. **H2: How do quotes differ, a request or a configuration?**
   Answers: What happens when a buyer wants a quote? ChatSKU: buyer submits a quote request in chat (name, email, company), team reviews in the dashboard, applies pricing and discounts, adds notes, sends back; discounts by customer group on paid plans; quotes export to CSV. Zoovu: self-service RFQ where buyers configure products, bundles or BOMs and request a quote; Visual CPQ with compatibility mapping, automatic quote generation, PDF configuration download; industrial CPQ with ERP pricing and approvals (per Zoovu's pages). Short fairness line: for configurable lines, Zoovu is the stronger fit. Link /rfq-automation-manufacturers/ for the request-and-review flow.

6. **H2: What does it take to launch, and what does it cost to evaluate?**
   Answers: How long, how much effort, can I try it? Zoovu: B2B Site Search page states an average launch of 6 weeks; AI search says "as little as six weeks"; services available; assistant is added by copying two lines of generated code (API-first). Pricing: product fee plus usage or experience-based fee, tailored; no prices published; free 30-minute demo, no trial mentioned. ChatSKU: one line of code, live in under a day, published plans on the pricing page (no amounts), free trial with usage limits (no day counts; excludes image search, customer groups and tiered pricing, integrations, analytics). Mention both timelines once each in plain words; the numbers appear in the table and here only. Citation link #2 to zoovu.com/pricing/. Link /chatsku-pricing/ and /signup/.
   Note: do NOT say "Zoovu takes six weeks to go live" as a flat claim. Say "Zoovu's B2B Site Search page states an average launch of 6 weeks (checked 2026-11-05)". The assistant's two-line install is a separate stated fact; mention it so the post is not one-sided.

7. **H2: When does Zoovu fit better, and when does ChatSKU?**
   Answers: Who should choose which? Heading must NOT use "Pick X when". Use two short prose paragraphs plus one honest overlap paragraph, and a closing "you may want both" note (a manufacturer with configurable lines can use a configurator and still want an after-hours catalog assistant). Zoovu fits better: configurable or compatibility-bound products, a full search, discovery and data layer, enterprise stacks (SAP), dealer syndication, SOC 2 Type 2 as a procurement need (Zoovu's claim; ChatSKU's pages state encryption, GDPR and DPAs, not SOC 2), willingness to run a multi-week project. ChatSKU fits better: catalogs in PDF/Excel/CSV or a plain site, the job is answering buyers and capturing quote requests including after hours, live in a day, free trial, published plans, no PIM or data team. Link /for-b2b-manufacturers-distributors-and-wholesalers/ and /ai-sales-assistant-b2b-ecommerce/ here or in section 4.
