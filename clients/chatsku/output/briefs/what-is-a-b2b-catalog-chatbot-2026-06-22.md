### Section 11: FAQ
**Background color:** `#f9f9fb`
**Word target:** 400-500 words (across 4-6 questions)
**H2 heading:** Frequently asked questions

Use H3 for each question. These questions are for category newcomers. Confirm at draft stage that none overlap post 294's FAQ set.

**H3: Is a B2B catalog chatbot the same as live chat or a sales rep?**
Answer: No. Live chat connects a buyer to a human. A B2B catalog chatbot is automated -- it reads your catalog data and answers product questions without a rep present. It does not replace the rep for complex deals, but it handles the initial qualification layer so your reps spend time on buyers who are already ready to move.

**H3: Do I need to rebuild my catalog or website to use one?**
Answer: Not if you pick the right tool. Some platforms ingest your existing catalog -- a PDF, an Excel file, an ERP export -- and go live without touching your website. Ask any vendor whether their setup requires a data migration or developer work before you commit.

**H3: Will it work if my catalog is just a PDF or a spreadsheet?**
Answer: It should, if the tool supports PDF and file-based ingestion. Not all do. If your catalog lives in a PDF or spreadsheet, confirm that the platform can index it before signing a contract. [See how PDF catalog chatbots work] -- link "PDF catalog chatbot" to `/pdf-catalog-chatbot/`

**H3: Does this replace my sales team?**
Answer: No. According to Gartner (May 2026), 69% of B2B buyers still turn to sales reps to validate AI-generated insights before making final purchasing decisions. The chatbot handles the catalog lookup and qualification. Your reps close the deal. Their time goes to buyers who are already ready to buy, not to answering availability questions that a database can answer in three seconds.

**H3: Is this only for large distributors, or does it work for smaller wholesalers too?**
Answer: Both. The common thread is catalog complexity and after-hours buyers, not company size. A $3M wholesale supplier with 8,000 SKUs and two sales reps has the same problem as a national distributor -- fewer reps, more catalog, buyers who expect answers at odd hours.

**H3: How is this different from the search bar on my website?**
Answer: Your search bar finds matching text. A B2B catalog chatbot understands intent, asks clarifying questions, applies your pricing logic, and generates a quote. A buyer typing "food grade fittings 1/2 inch" into a search bar gets a list of results to scroll. The same buyer asking an AI catalog assistant gets a specific answer, a price for their account, and an offer to build a quote.
