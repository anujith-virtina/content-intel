## Thesis

> Buying a chat widget does not put a B2B catalog at Era 07: the 9 Eras tool scores Era 07 only "partly" machine-readable because answering a human in seconds and exposing a catalog to another machine are two different engineering problems, and most sellers who think they've arrived have only solved the first one.

## Why this, why now, why us

- **Why this angle:** it's the single most useful, least-explained fact on the tool's own page (Era 07 scores `part`, not `yes`, on machine-readability) and no existing post explains why. It also gives the tool a long-form home instead of leaving the insight trapped in a dropdown.
- **Why now:** the tool went live and needs a companion piece that ranks independently and feeds it traffic; Era 08 ("the one worth watching," per the tool's own closing line) is where the market is heading and almost nobody is there yet (commercetools: under 5% of B2B orgs are AI-ready).
- **Why this client:** ChatSKU's product is explicitly built to close this exact gap (catalog-grounded answers, tier pricing, quote workflows) without pretending the job is finished at "we have a chat window now."

## Audience

Ops and sales leaders at B2B manufacturers, distributors, and wholesalers ($1M-$50M revenue, 10-200 employees) who already bought or evaluated some form of AI chat in the last two years. They know their catalog and their competitors' speed advantage. They don't know that "AI chat" and "machine-readable catalog" are different technical claims, or that the difference is exactly what determines whether an AI agent shortlists them in 2027.

### H2: Why does the tool score Era 07 only "partly" machine-readable? (~380 words — the spine, give this the most room)

- Direct answer, first sentence: because answering a human in natural language and exposing a catalog so another machine can query it deterministically are two different engineering problems, and Era 07 only solves the first.
- Explain in plain terms (no acronym-soup): a conversational layer retrieves relevant catalog passages and phrases a natural answer for a person. That's flexible and fast, but it is not the same as a structured feed or API that another piece of software can query without a human-phrased conversation in the middle.
- Name the practical middle path once: many teams end up combining structured fields (for facts like price and stock) with retrieval (for phrasing and context). Keep this at the level an ops leader needs, not a developer.
- **Link placement**: `/how-ai-product-search-works/`, anchor **"AI product search mechanics"**, framed as "the retrieval mechanics behind this, if you want the deeper technical version" — hand off, do not re-explain embeddings/RAG/RRF here.
- This is where you name the Era 07-to-08 stakes: Era 08 requires the same catalog to be queryable by another agent, with no human-phrased conversation in between.
- Don't include: any protocol acronym here (save ACP/AP2/MCP/A2A for the next section, at most one or two, with a handoff link).
