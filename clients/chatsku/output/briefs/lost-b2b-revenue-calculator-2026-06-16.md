**The 5-step formula (present in this exact order):**

Step 1: Monthly inbound leads x percentage arriving after hours = after-hours leads
Step 2: After-hours leads x percentage that don't receive a same-session response = abandoned leads
Step 3: Abandoned leads x average quote value = revenue at risk per month (before close rate)
Step 4: Revenue at risk x your historical close rate = monthly lost revenue
Step 5: Monthly lost revenue x 12 = annual revenue gap

**Worked example (use these exact numbers — labeled clearly as illustrative):**

- 52% arrive after hours = 26 after-hours leads
- 70% abandonment rate (no same-session response) = 18 abandoned leads
- Average quote value: $4,200
- Close rate: 28%
- Monthly lost revenue: 18 x $4,200 x 28% = $21,168
- Annual gap: $21,168 x 12 = $254,016

**Q: How do I track after-hours lead loss?**
A: Start in your CRM or analytics platform. Filter inbound leads by time of submission and compare close rates between leads that arrived during business hours versus those that came in after 5pm. If your CRM doesn't capture submission time, check your web form logs or contact your marketing automation platform. Most companies discover they have no system for this — which is itself the answer.

**Q: Does after-hours lead loss affect B2B more than B2C?**
A: Yes. B2C buyers often self-purchase immediately. B2B buyers need a quote, pricing confirmation, or MOQ check before committing.
