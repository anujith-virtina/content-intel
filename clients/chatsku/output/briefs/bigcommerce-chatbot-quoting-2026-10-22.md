## 11. People also ask: the 4 H3 questions

Each H3 is a real question. Each answer is 1 to 2 sentences, direct, then at most one short supporting sentence. Do not repeat these answers elsewhere in the body.

1. **H3: Is a quote form or live chat better for B2B quote requests?**
   Planned answer: Neither wins on its own. A form is better for custom work where the buyer knows what they need, live chat is better when a person is online, and both leave gaps on price correctness and after-hours coverage.
2. **H3: Can a chatbot build a B2B quote with the customer's own pricing?**
   Planned answer: Yes, but only if it reads that customer's price list or customer group. Without live price data it quotes list price, which is worse than no assistant.
3. **H3: What happens to a quote request that arrives after hours?**
   Planned answer: With a form, it queues until a rep opens it. With live chat, it usually goes unanswered. An AI catalog assistant can finish the request on the spot and leave a complete package for morning.
4. **H3: Can a chatbot show the right price for each customer group?**
   Planned answer: Yes, if it is connected to the customer group and price list data on the store. It should show the group's price, and flag anything it cannot confirm for a rep.

Overlap guard: PAA 2 and 4 are close. Keep 2 about building the whole quote (items, quantity, price, handoff) and 4 about showing a single price to a recognised buyer. ## 13. Illustrative scenario (labelled "Illustrative example")

Label exactly: **Illustrative example.** Use a short bold lead-in or italic label so it cannot be mistaken for a client story. No named client, no results, no figures presented as outcomes, no percentage uplift.

Setup: a distributor on BigCommerce with company accounts and a negotiated price list. At 8pm on a weeknight, a buyer for an established customer wants a mixed order: several SKUs in different pack sizes plus one item the buyer is not sure the store carries.

- **Quote form:** the buyer fills in what they know, leaves the uncertain item as a free-text note, and submits. The rep opens it the next morning, finds a missing quantity and a price that needs checking against the company's list, and has to write back. The first real conversation starts at 9am.
- **Live chat:** the widget shows offline. The buyer either emails or leaves.
- **AI catalog assistant:** the buyer types the order in plain words. The assistant matches SKUs, asks the one missing quantity, applies the company's price list, and notes the uncertain item for a rep. The rep's morning queue holds a complete, priced draft and one flagged question.

Close with one line: the difference is not speed alone. It is what the rep opens first. Do not claim win rates, time saved or revenue.
